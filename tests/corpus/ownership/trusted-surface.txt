# The trusted surface of ownership words (docs/design/ownership-checker.md
# §24.6): every declaration of core or a platform that carries ownership
# words, the package whose ownership dump prints it, the fixture under
# tests/corpus/ that pins it, and the flips that pin must notice.
#
# A flip is what `beni dump --stage=ownership --ownership-flips=` takes:
# `<slot>:<from>><to>[+…]`, a slot a parameter from 1 or `res`, a word or
# `-`, several separated by `|`. Each is the declaration's words moved in the
# UNSAFE direction — claiming less of an argument, or more of the result —
# and `check_test.zig` runs the fixture under each and fails unless the
# report changes: the fixture's golden would fail if the word were wrong
# that way. `-` says the words have no unsafer neighbour a verdict can see,
# and why is in the comment beside the row. A fifth column names what the
# fixture must mention when the declaration is not `pub`.
#
