**What I learned.** Every route answered on the first run under Miniflare; what did not was my own fixture idea. A request parked on a wait no host operation of its own answers — a `Deferred` another request completes — is cancelled by workerd as hung: coordination between requests goes through an object, and the per-request scheduler is for a request's own I/O. Under Miniflare the Worker's client is Miniflare's entry worker, so a client that leaves, through `dispatchFetch`'s signal or a raw connection destroyed, never reaches `request.signal`; that half of #600 is owed to `wrangler dev`.
