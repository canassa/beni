## 2026-10-30 15:59 CET — own2: comparisons are read through their components (#1324 review)

**What I did.** The review of #1324 found three holes in "a comparison written in beni outside core is never derived". (1) A derived row (`Wrap` over `Key`) was derived whenever its context evidence was not hand-written, but its field comparisons are not evidence, so `Wrap (Key xs) < …` was accepted. (2) A container comparison in core (`List.compare`) applied its published class, or the vacuous evidence check (a top/ext term keeps its evidence in the site, not in its args). (3) `embedded` is not core: platforms are embedded and `--core-root` is not. Now `Groups.callee` builds a derived, primitive or core-container comparison from the classes of the user-written `eq`/`compare` its first argument's places reach (`componentClasses`, over the place graph); one with no class leaves the call unbuilt; evidence that is a parameter or a codec still does. `Rows.derivedClass` now carries a component's `W` use, not only `E`. Core is `Session.fileIsCore`. Fixtures: `ownership/DerivedOverWriter{Imported,Third}`, `ContainerOverWriter{,Local}`, `ContainerOverUnbuiltEq`, `DerivedOverUnbuiltEq`, and a build test for `--core-root`. Goldens: eight `run/` `.v2.unchecked` files change, mostly by losing lines (callers now checked against composed classes).

**What I learned.** A type's methods are one namespace per module, so a single-module derived variant cannot exist (`Wrap` and `Key` cannot both have `compare` there). `Class.writes` is the unknown-top flag, not "writes": a component's `W` has to be read off its param uses.
