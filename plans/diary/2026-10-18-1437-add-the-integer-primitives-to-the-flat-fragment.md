**What I learned.** A fragment constructor whose primitive is a variable (`.prim op [a, b]`) blocks the definitional unfolding of `uses` and `usesWith`, because their match tests `prepend` and `close` first. Case on the primitive predicate before `apply` or `simp`, or use the equation `uses_scalar`. A draft module that imports the built `FlatFragment.Trace` checks a new case in about a minute; the chain rebuild after a change to `Support.lean` takes much longer, so draft first.
