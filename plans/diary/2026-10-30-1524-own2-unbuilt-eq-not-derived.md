## 2026-10-30 15:24 CET — own2: an unbuilt hand-written eq is not a derived comparison

**What I did.** #1320. Under `--ownership=v2`, `==` on a user `eq` whose walk was not built (or was stopped at a limit) was read as the derived, read-only comparison, so a caller that kept a list beside a writing `eq` was checked and accepted. `Groups.Module.callee` now takes the derived fallback only for a comparison declared in core (embedded modules, plumbed as `Input.embedded`) or a non-beni one; any other beni-written `eq`/`compare` without a class stays unbuilt, so its caller is unchecked. `<`/`compare` go through the same code. Fixtures: `ownership/UnbuiltEq`, `ownership/UnbuiltEqImported`, and a build_test for an imported `eq` stopped at a limit (a corpus case that reaches a limit is refused by the harness). Three run/ `.v2.unchecked` goldens gained the callers of hand-written `eq` that are now unchecked. One sentence added to ownership-v2.md §2.13 item 13.

**What I learned.** The first cut (any `.value` decl) broke `List.==`: core's container comparisons are beni-written with no words and rely on the derived path, so "hand-written" needs the core/user line, not the declaration kind. A limit-stopped walk cannot be a corpus fixture.
