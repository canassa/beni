## 2026-10-26 21:19 CET — The κ trie

**What I did.** #1073 with option A. A κ snapshot is now a trie of positions: conditions by node, links by step, paths relative to the root of the snapshot that stores them. A construction links its children's snapshots. A read walks its steps. The frontier is sparse: the steps the structure names plus the snapshots' links. A walk over a cyclic shape folds its output where the shape cycles. I moved every reader together, and also the call graft, read-off, the normal form, the printer and the block codec. The block now records what the snapshot is at each position, and the reader rebuilds the trie by route. §2.9, §3.4 and §3.6 are rewritten first. Filed #1112: a recursive tower is quadratic on master too, because a literal's propagate re-walks its member.

**What I learned.** Canonical ⟲ paths cannot key a linked trie: folding depends on the prefix. Positions must be the steps a read takes. A block reader cannot rebuild the trie by merging cursors, because the writer's refinement shares equal positions across routes, which would join two trie positions' entries. Grouping by route is exact. Ctor steps are interned per table, so a test that reads a decoded class must build its steps in the reader's table.
