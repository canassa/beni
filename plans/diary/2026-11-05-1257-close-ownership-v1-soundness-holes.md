## 2026-11-05 12:57 CET — Close ownership v1 soundness holes

**What I did.** Closed #1751, #1959 and v1's side of #1956 in `src/own/` (PR #1983). A callback's `need` reaches the caller's function parameter however it is called. A taken supply keeps its container's conditions through a lambda. A split element stops being apart from its element cell once something other than its own pattern reaches that cell. A closure's write of a capture meets later reads through any holder. The adversarial review found two live holes in my first version (a split inside a closure, a local function reading a later binding) and a false refusal from exposure leaking across a local group's rounds. All three are fixed and pinned. Filed #1982: the same family as #1751 with a lambda instead of the parameter, still open.

**What I learned.** v1 cannot tell a pattern's rest from its list, because they are the same cells. So the #1956 fix tracks what was read, and it refuses some safe programs: `[ el, …xs ]` then its tail, a read of `l`'s length beside `[ …rest, p ]`, an alias of the rest. Taken cells are named per frame, so every comparison in a lambda has to go through the frames that bind them. A local function's body is walked before its block's bindings, so a check at the demand misses them. `perl` with `|` as the delimiter turns `\|` into alternation, and it corrupted the first line of a 22 000-line file.
