**What I learned.** A need has two halves to make complete, not one: the reading of the code it is discharged against, and every way the closure that carries it can travel. Matching `instAt`'s reading entry by entry, and asking what a leaf forgets on the way, found more than another shape test would have. A golden that no gate reads is no test.
