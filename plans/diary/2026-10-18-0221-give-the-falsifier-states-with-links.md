## 2026-10-18 02:21 CEST — give the falsifier states with links

**What I did.** Issue #274 (Part of #165). `Falsify.config` now holds a link in a third of its configurations: a view of a nonempty leaf at the next address, `front` or back, half of those a chain of two views; the view is named by its source's origins, and variables are bound to the view (its abstract carries the source's origins and a fresh site, as the checker's `.view` rule gives it) or to the source. `expr` generates `.view`, `writeExpr` pushes through a fresh view, the tally counts configurations and post-states with links, and `rename` names a view by its source's new origins. 20,000 trials: 6,320 linked pre-states, 4,285 linked accepted post-states, no hypothesis false, no observational failure, no naming failure.

**What I learned.** The first run failed on four configurations where the expression made a second view of a protected source and dropped it: `rename` named only arrays with requirements or an old name, so the garbage view had no origins and the component was named apart. The step lemma's rule already says a new view is named by its source's origins, whether or not a binding describes it; the falsifier's construction had to say the same. A clause about every array of a component is tested only when the generator makes arrays nobody holds.
