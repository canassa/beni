**What I learned.** A level below one route folds onto another route's arrival, so reading routes one after the other gives two leaves of one slot, and `disjoint` refuses a program that holds `k` once. The first attempt regressed exactly the accepted twins for it. A `⟲` of a contract does not say how far the callee went: `wp`, which stops at `Wrap`, and `wr` write one slot, so a safe narrow callee is refused at every route now, as it already was at the shortest. On a type that doubles on its cycle the read costs 2^d more, the ratio of slots named: canonical names carry the route, so a read cannot share below the count of slots it answers. Sharing further needs a symbol for a level's set, a design change, not this fix.
